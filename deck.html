Act 5 · BusinessHonest ledger

What is unfinished, and the gaps I found myself.

Unfinished

  • Agentic evidence mapper is on a branch. Production main runs the older engines behind a flag
  • The one-engine script-builder consolidation has not merged
  • Pentest results are a lab run on local runtimes
  • Agentic vendor evaluator is off by default. 10 of 97 golden cases still fail
  • Telemetry and metering exist as plans only

Gaps I found and flagged

  • NO_MATCH does not yet distinguish a correct abstention from a failure
  • Liveness stays green when a worker thread dies. I saw it twice
  • Partial extraction lowers recall silently. Nothing alerts on it yet
  • Generated-code isolation is the next thing I would harden
  • Secrets rotation lagged behind speed. Now tracked by type in a risk register
